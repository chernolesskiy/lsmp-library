# Sources

Credits and links that were free text at the top of the chess.com PGN files, moved here so they stay with
their collection. Keyed by collection file name (without `.pgn`). Text is verbatim from the original file.
Every collection comes from the chess.com library export of 2026-10-05; per-game `Source`, `Annotator` and `GameURL` tags
stay inside the PGN headers.

**General credits**

- **Lichess puzzle database** (https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles), licence: CC0 1.0. Puzzle positions, ids, ratings and themes in every puzzle sheet and puzzle data file. CC0 asks for no credit; it is credited here anyway. Each puzzle on a sheet shows its Lichess id (#li/...).
- **Lichess studies and their authors** (https://lichess.org), licence: set by each author. Some collections include games and comments taken from public Lichess studies. Their authors stay credited in the PGN tags Annotator, StudyName and ChapterURL of each game.
- **chess.com** (https://www.chess.com), licence: chess.com terms of use. The collections come from an export of LSMP's chess.com library made on 2026-10-05. Game records and lesson material there are credited in the PGN tags (Source, GameURL) and in the per-collection entries below.
- **LSMP hint generation**, licence: CC BY-NC 4.0. The hints printed on the puzzle sheets come from LSMP's rule-based hint generator, which derives every hint from the puzzle's solution line. During development it used python-chess (GPL-3.0) and the Stockfish engine (GPL-3.0) to check hints; neither is included in this library.
- **Ghostscript and Nimbus Sans** (https://ghostscript.com), licence: AGPL-3.0. The PDF sheets were rewritten with Ghostscript 10.05.1 to reduce their size. Their text is set in Nimbus Sans, a Helvetica-compatible typeface by URW++ that Ghostscript embeds as a subset.
- **Board and piece artwork in the PDF sheets**, licence: not covered by this library's licence. The board diagrams printed on the puzzle sheets were drawn with third-party picture sets of boards and pieces. That artwork is not LSMP's work and is not covered by the CC BY-NC 4.0 licence of this library.
